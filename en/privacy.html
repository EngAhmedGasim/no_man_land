Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors and clients across our Riyadh, Dubai, and Khartoum offices. This page explains what data we collect and how it's used.

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Al-Qasimi & Partners Law Firm collects, uses, and protects your data when you visit our website or contact us by email, WhatsApp, or our contact form. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email, phone number, and the content of your inquiry — when you contact us through the contact form, email, or WhatsApp, along with limited technical data (browser type, pages visited) collected automatically to help us improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, assess whether we can assist with your legal matter, communicate with you about your request, and improve our website's content and experience. We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Confidentiality & the Attorney-Client Relationship

Any information you share with us regarding a legal inquiry is treated in strict confidence in line with standard professional practice. Please note that submitting the contact form or messaging us by email or WhatsApp does not, by itself, create a formal attorney-client relationship unless and until confirmed in a separate written engagement.

Cookies

This website may use essential cookies to function correctly, and analytics cookies to help us understand how visitors use the site. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though some parts of the site may not work as intended as a result.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We may share limited data with trusted service providers (such as hosting or email providers) only to the extent necessary to run the website and serve you, and may disclose information where required by a court order or applicable law in any country where we operate.

Your Rights

You may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it where legally possible, by contacting us at the email address below.

Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or for the period required by applicable law in the relevant country, whichever is longer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons. Any update will be posted on this page along with its last-revised date.

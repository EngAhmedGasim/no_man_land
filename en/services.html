Services

Eight practice areas, complete coverage

From your first contract to your most complex dispute — here is our practice in detail.

Corporate & Commercial Law

We stand beside your business from incorporation through growth and merger — choosing the right legal structure and drafting governance and commercial documents.

Our corporate team acts as an ongoing legal partner, not a firm you call only in a crisis. We start by studying the business model and choosing the right legal form (limited-liability company, closed joint-stock company, or sole establishment), then draft incorporation documents and internal governance policies aligned with corporate law and international best practice. As clients expand across Riyadh, Dubai, and Khartoum, we account for the differences between each jurisdiction so the group's legal structure stays consistent. We also review supply, distribution, and franchise agreements, and lead M&A transactions from due diligence through closing.

  • Company formation & structuring
  • Drafting & reviewing commercial contracts
  • Corporate governance & compliance
  • Mergers & acquisitions

Litigation & Advocacy

Our team represents clients before commercial and general courts and courts of appeal, building an evidence-driven case strategy.

Before accepting any matter, our team gives a candid assessment of the likely outcome, cost, and timeline, so the client decides with full clarity. We draft statements of claim and defence memoranda in precise legal language backed by evidence, and argue cases before commercial and general courts and courts of appeal and cassation. We also stay on through the post-judgment stage — enforcement and precautionary attachment — because winning a case is only half the job. For cross-border disputes touching our three offices, we coordinate representation across jurisdictions through our professional network.

  • Representation before commercial & general courts
  • Drafting pleadings & legal memoranda
  • Judgment enforcement follow-up
  • Appeals & cassation
Contact us about this service

Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

A faster, more confidential route to resolving commercial disputes through local and international arbitration, including advocacy before the SCCA.

An arbitration outcome depends as much on the quality of the case file as on the strength of the argument, so we prepare the request for arbitration and its exhibits with particular care from day one. Our team appears before the Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration (SCCA) — the non-profit body established in 2014 to administer arbitration and mediation to international standards in Arabic and English — as well as the regional and international centers relevant to our Dubai and Khartoum offices. Where it serves the client, we explore mediation and amicable settlement first to save time and cost, while keeping full arbitration available if escalation becomes necessary.

  • Domestic & international arbitration
  • Advocacy before the SCCA
  • Mediation & amicable settlement
  • Drafting arbitration clauses

Family & Personal Status

We handle personal status matters with care — from marriage contracts and divorce to custody, alimony, and inheritance.

Family matters carry a human dimension that matters as much as the legal one, so every file begins with an unhurried conversation to understand the full picture before recommending a path forward. We help draft and notarize marriage contracts, represent clients in divorce and khula proceedings in a way that protects their rights, and handle custody and alimony with the flexibility that puts children's interests first. In inheritance matters we handle estate inventories and distribution according to the applicable shares, and notarize wills and powers of attorney to head off disputes among heirs. We also handle cross-border family files where relatives hold residency or assets across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Sudan.

  • Marriage & divorce cases
  • Custody & alimony
  • Estate division & inheritance
  • Notarizing powers of attorney
Contact us about this service

Real Estate Law

From preliminary contracts to title deeds and mortgages, we secure your real-estate transactions and represent you in lease and development disputes.

Whether you are buying a residential unit or developing a multi-phase real-estate project, most property disputes trace back to a contract that was not reviewed carefully enough at the start. We review and refine sale, lease, and development contracts before signing, verify that title deeds are free of conflicting liens or rights in rem, and support real-estate financing and mortgage arrangements with lenders. When a dispute arises over handover, workmanship, or contract termination, we represent clients before the relevant courts and administrative bodies. For developer clients in Dubai and Riyadh, we also handle the licensing needed to bring a project to market and ongoing compliance with local real-estate regulations.

  • Sale & lease contract review
  • Title deeds & mortgages
  • Lease & development disputes
  • Real-estate project licensing
Contact us about this service

Labor & Employment Law

We help employers and employees understand their rights under the Labor Law and represent both sides before labor dispute bodies.

We work with employers to draft employment contracts and internal work regulations that balance management flexibility with employee rights, and review termination and compensation policies to reduce the risk of future disputes. When a dispute arises — whether unfair dismissal or a pay claim — we represent both employers and employees before the competent labor dispute bodies. We also help multi-country employers stay compliant with local workforce-nationalization requirements in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, updating internal policies whenever the regulations change.

  • Employment contracts & regulations
  • Termination & compensation
  • Representation before labor dispute bodies
  • Localization & labor-law compliance
Contact us about this service

Bankruptcy & Restructuring

Proactive solutions for financially distressed businesses under the applicable bankruptcy laws, preserving continuity before liquidation.

Early financial distress doesn't have to mean liquidation if a business acts in time. We help clients assess their financial position candidly and choose the right path — preventive settlement, restructuring, or, only when necessary, orderly liquidation. We negotiate with creditors to reschedule obligations, whether under court supervision or privately, and work to protect a distressed company's core assets so it can keep operating. Our experience spans Saudi Arabia and the UAE, where bankruptcy tracks and conditions differ, which we factor into advice for cross-border groups.

  • Preventive settlement & restructuring
  • Bankruptcy & liquidation procedures
  • Creditor negotiations
  • Asset protection during distress
Contact us about this service

Intellectual Property & Trademarks

We protect your intangible assets by registering trademarks and patents with the competent authorities.

Your trademark or invention is an asset to register, protect, and value before someone else copies it — not after. We handle search and registration with the Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) and the equivalent UAE bodies, from prior-art searches through the issued certificate, with ongoing renewal tracking. When infringement or counterfeiting occurs, we move quickly with formal cease-and-desist notices and litigation to stop the violation and protect our client's rights. We also draft licensing, technology-transfer, and franchise agreements with terms that protect the brand's long-term value.

  • Trademark & patent registration
  • Copyright & design protection
  • Infringement & counterfeiting cases
  • Licensing & technology-transfer agreements

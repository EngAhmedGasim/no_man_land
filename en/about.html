About Us

The story of a firm built on trust

Since 2011, we've guided individuals and companies through their most consequential legal decisions, pairing regulatory precision with genuinely personal follow-through across our offices in Riyadh, Dubai, and Khartoum.

Our Story

A simple beginning, a clear vision

Al-Qasimi & Partners Law Firm started in Riyadh with a small team focused on commercial law, built on the belief that clients deserve plain explanations, not legal jargon.

Today the firm has grown into a multi-disciplinary team covering eight core practice areas — without losing its close, personal attention to every client and file.

We later expanded beyond Saudi Arabia with offices in Dubai and Khartoum, so clients with cross-border transactions or disputes are served by one team familiar with all three jurisdictions, rather than juggling several independent law firms.

Vision & Mission

What guides our work

Our Vision

To be the first choice in legal counsel for companies and individuals across the Kingdom, through precise advice that supports confident decisions.

Our Mission

We help clients clearly understand their rights and obligations, and represent them faithfully before courts and arbitration bodies.

Our Values

Integrity, absolute confidentiality, legal precision, and strict punctuality — values we never compromise on.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way
